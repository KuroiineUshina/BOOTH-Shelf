# Paperlogy

- Official page: https://freesentation.blog/paperlogyfont
- Official repository: https://github.com/Freesentation/paperlogy
- Source archive: `Paperlogy-1.000.zip` (Korean and Latin edition)
- Included weights: Regular 400, Medium 500, SemiBold 600, Bold 700, ExtraBold 800
- Retrieved: 2026-07-25
- License: SIL Open Font License 1.1

The font files are copied unchanged from the official archive. Paperlogy's
Korean glyphs are based on Gmarket Sans and its Latin glyphs are based on
Montserrat. Japanese UI uses the separately bundled M PLUS 1 font.
